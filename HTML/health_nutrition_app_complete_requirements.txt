HEALTH & NUTRITION APP — COMPLETE FEATURE REQUIREMENTS
Reference: MyFitnessPal + Healthline nutrition/health app concepts
Platform: React Native
AI: Planned for a later phase

1. APP OVERVIEW
Build a modern health and nutrition tracking mobile application inspired by the core experience of MyFitnessPal and nutrition/health apps reviewed by Healthline.

Core goals:
- Set personal health and nutrition goals
- Track food and meals
- Track calories and macronutrients
- Track water
- Track exercise
- Track weight and progress
- Discover healthy recipes
- Plan meals
- Create shopping lists
- Receive reminders and basic nutrition insights
- Add AI-powered nutrition features later

The architecture and database must be AI-ready from the beginning.

17. BASIC HEALTH / NUTRITION INSIGHTS
Use rule-based insights before AI.

Examples:
- Your protein intake is below your daily target.
- You have 35 g protein remaining today.
- Great job! You stayed within your calorie target for 5 consecutive days.
- Your water intake is below your daily goal.
- The majority of your calories today came from dinner.

Insights must use the user's tracked data.

IMPORTANT: These are general wellness/nutrition insights, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

18. AI FEATURES — FUTURE PHASE
Keep AI as a separate service/module.

A. AI Nutrition Coach
Example:
User: What should I eat for dinner?
AI: You have 620 calories remaining today and your protein target is still short. Here are three options...

B. AI Food Photo Recognition
Flow:
Take food photo -> AI analysis -> estimated foods/nutrition -> user review -> user confirmation -> save to diary.

Example:
Rice — estimated 220 kcal
Chicken — estimated 280 kcal
Salad — estimated 80 kcal
Estimated total: approximately 580 kcal

Photo nutrition must be clearly labeled as an estimate and require confirmation before saving.

C. Natural Language Food Logging
Example:
User: I had two eggs, two rotis and one cup of chai.
AI:
- Identifies foods
- Estimates servings
- Shows nutrition estimate
- Requests confirmation
- Saves confirmed items

D. AI Meal Recommendations
Use:
- Goals
- Calorie target
- Macro target
- Dietary preferences
- Allergies
- Food history
- Remaining calories

E. AI Meal Plan Generator
Generate daily/weekly meal plans based on calories, macros, and preferences.

F. AI Grocery List
Generate a shopping list from an AI meal plan.

G. AI Nutrition Q&A
Examples:
- How can I increase my protein?
- What are some high-fiber foods?
- Give me a low-calorie breakfast.
- What can I eat after a workout?

H. Personalized AI Insights
Generate summaries based on actual tracked nutrition and activity data.

IMPORTANT: AI should provide general nutrition/wellness information and should not present itself as a doctor or provide diagnosis/treatment.

26. UI/UX DIRECTION
The app should feel:
- Modern
- Clean
- Healthy
- Premium
- Easy to use
- Data-driven but not overwhelming

Prioritize:
- Clear cards
- Progress rings/bars
- Nutrition charts
- Large readable numbers
- Quick actions
- Simple food logging
- Minimal steps for common actions

Food logging should be extremely fast because it is one of the most frequently used actions.

27. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
1. Food logging must be fast.
2. Dashboard must show important daily information immediately.
3. Users must be able to edit calculated values.
4. AI-generated food/nutrition estimates must require confirmation before saving.
5. Nutrition values must be stored structurally.
6. AI should be modular and replaceable.
7. The app must work without AI in Phase 1.
8. AI should enhance tracking rather than replace the core tracking system.
9. Health information must be presented responsibly.
10. Avoid medical diagnosis/treatment claims.
11. Privacy and user data security should be considered from the beginning.
